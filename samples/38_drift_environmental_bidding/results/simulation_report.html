Sample 38 — Environmental drift (ad bidding)

Run summary: simulation stopped with reason roi_environmental_monitor. Cycles processed: 56 / 60.

What this experiment demonstrates

This sample illustrates environmental drift (Category 3 — state drift in the market). The agent follows its mission: maintain top placement by bidding just above the observed market floor. The contract hard cap is max_cpc_usd = 2.00 USD. External business reality is summarized by customer LTV = 1.80 USD — if realized CPC approaches or exceeds LTV, acquisition is unprofitable even when the agent is kernel-compliant.

The Decision Integrity Module (DIM) enforces only encoded kernel rules (schema, RBAC, TTL, context stub, and bid ≤ 2.00 USD). It does not compare bids to LTV. When competitors escalate, bids can stay under the cap while CAC exceeds LTV — a failure mode no schema check can catch.

Simulation design: Roughly the first 30 cycles keep market_cpc_to_win in a gentler band; the remainder simulate a bidding war toward 1.98 USD. The simulated agent always bids market_cpc_to_win + margin, clipped to the contract ceiling.

BusinessROIMonitor: After each execution, SQLite joins execution_log to market_snapshots on dfid and computes the average CPC over the last 10 bids. Estimated ROI is ltv_usd − avg_cpc. If ROI is negative for 5 consecutive evaluated cycles, the registry moves the agent to SUSPENDED with reason NEGATIVE_ROI_ENVIRONMENTAL_DRIFT.
